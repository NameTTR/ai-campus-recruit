# v2.4 Gateway Trusted Identity

## Scope

- Business services now prefer gateway-injected `X-User-Id` and `X-User-Role` for student/company owned operations.
- Request-provided `studentId` and `companyId` are still accepted when identity headers are absent, so every service remains independently runnable for local demos and tests.
- `ADMIN` calls keep query-parameter based cross-tenant access for review and management screens.

## Covered Flows

- Student identity:
  - `GET /api/students/profile`
  - `PUT /api/students/profile`
  - `POST /api/resumes/upload`
  - `POST /api/matches/resume-job`
  - `GET /api/matches/student/{studentId}`
  - `POST /api/deliveries`
  - `GET /api/deliveries/my`
  - `POST /api/ai/interview/questions`
  - `POST /api/ai/interview/feedback`
  - `GET /api/ai/interview/records`
- Company identity:
  - `POST /api/jobs`
  - `GET /api/deliveries/company`
  - `POST /api/ai/candidates/screen`
  - `GET /api/ai/candidates/screenings`
